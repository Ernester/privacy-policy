English


Diya provides a private symbolic prayer space and a japa counter. This version does not require an account. It stores your selected deity, language, lamp/flower state, repetition count, practice dates, intention note and preferences in local application storage to provide these features. The application does not upload this information to an operator server and includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK for optional rewarded advertisements. Google may process device identifiers, IP/network information, diagnostics and ad interactions under its own policies. Test ads also involve third-party processing. Diya does not include your note, deity choice or practice record as advertising request parameters. Production advertising follows the configured Google UMP consent flow; ad privacy options are available in Settings when required.

The bundled bell and three instrumental/environmental soundscapes play offline. Ad requests require a network connection. The app does not request microphone, contacts or location access. Local notes are not encrypted by Diya itself. Device security and operating system backup/transfer behaviour may affect who can access or restore locally stored information. The Android application requests that automatic backup be disabled; this does not remove any pre-existing backups or guarantee all manufacturer transfer behaviour.

Use Settings > Delete local data to remove Diya's local settings, note, count and practice history, or erase the note and save to clear only the note. Data stays locally until you remove it or uninstall the app, subject to your platform's storage and backup behaviour. Uninstalling or clearing storage may permanently remove your data. There is no cloud account to restore it from.

Google Play and the device operating system may process installation, purchase history (if applicable to the platform), diagnostics and account information under their own policies. This version does not implement in-app purchases. If you contact the operator, information you choose to send will be processed to respond to your request. Support messages are used to handle your request and are normally deleted within 90 days after resolution, unless retention is required for a legal obligation or dispute. To request deletion, email yunlaikeji@gmail.com.

Any future change to advertising or addition of cloud sync, calendar APIs, analytics or payments must be reflected in this policy before release. For questions, contact the operator using the address above.
